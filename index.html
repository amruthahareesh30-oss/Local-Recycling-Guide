Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why should we recycle?

Recycling reduces waste, saves natural resources and helps protect the environment.

2. Can plastic bottles be recycled?

Many plastic bottles can be recycled. Empty and clean them first, and check local recycling rules.

3. What is e-waste?

E-waste includes discarded electronic devices such as computers, mobile phones and batteries.

4. What is composting?

Composting is the process of converting suitable organic waste into nutrient-rich material.

5. How can I find recycling facilities?

You can use our facility directory and select your location to view available entries.
